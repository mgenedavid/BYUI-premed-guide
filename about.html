About This Platform

Built to make the pre-med path at BYU-Idaho clearer and more manageable.

Why This Exists

Pre-med can feel overwhelming—especially when you're trying to figure out which classes matter, how to balance semesters without burning out, and how to stay on track for the MCAT. This platform was built because that uncertainty shouldn't be the norm.

What This Helps You Do

Use student ratings to understand course difficulty before you enroll. Plan your semesters with a visual roadmap. See how courses connect to the MCAT. Make more informed decisions using the experiences of students who've already been there.

Our Hope for You

We hope this platform helps you feel more confident in your path, make better decisions, and avoid unnecessary stress along the way. We wish you the best as you move forward toward medical school.
