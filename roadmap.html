Road to Medical School

See how undergraduate planning, the MCAT, applications, interviews, medical school, and residency fit together.

01 · Undergraduate

Build the academic foundation

Complete your degree requirements while planning medical-school prerequisites, developing study habits, and exploring medicine through meaningful experiences.

02 · Experiences

Learn what medicine actually looks like

Clinical exposure, service, shadowing, research, employment, leadership, and teaching can all help you understand your goals and develop as a person.

03 · MCAT

Prepare deliberately

Choose a target date that fits your coursework and application plan. Build dedicated preparation time rather than treating MCAT study like another ordinary class.

04 · Application

Organize the story and the logistics

School research, letters, activities, personal statement, transcripts, primary application, secondaries, and costs all need their own timeline.

05 · Interviews & Decisions

Evaluate fit both ways

Interview preparation is about communicating clearly and learning enough to make your own informed school decisions.

06 · Medical School

Begin medical training

The exact curriculum varies by school, but medical school typically combines foundational science, clinical training, licensing examinations, and preparation for residency.

07 · Residency

Train in a specialty

Residency follows medical school and provides supervised specialty training. Fellowship may follow for additional subspecialty training.

Timelines and requirements vary by student and institution. Use this roadmap to understand the process, then verify current requirements with official university, testing, and application sources.
